What it means

55%

of sellers who signed a deal never listed a product that actually sold. The bottleneck isn't getting sellers to say yes. It's what happens to them after onboarding.

Channel quality varies 5×

Paid and organic search convert leads to deals at ~12%; email sits at 3%. Worth deprioritising or reworking email as an acquisition channel.

Onboarding gap is category-specific

Bags and household categories convert deal to sale at 60% or more. Food supplements and computers sit near 30%. Onboarding support could target these categories first.
